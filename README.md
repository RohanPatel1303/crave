# Crave

Crave is a food discovery platform designed to help users find restaurants, dishes, and dining experiences that match their tastes, mood, and location. The platform makes it easier to explore local options, compare choices, and discover new favorites through a simple, engaging, and personalized experience.

Built with convenience and discovery in mind, Crave connects people with food they are likely to love while supporting restaurants by making their offerings more visible to the right audience. By combining intuitive browsing, curated recommendations, and a seamless user experience, Crave transforms everyday meal decisions into a more enjoyable and satisfying experience.
